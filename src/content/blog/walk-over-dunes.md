date: 2026-09-14T14:39:45.197Z

This morning, I went to school for my first day of class

Later, Grandfather Tue called to ask if Grandma wanted to go to the village with him

She said yes

This afternoon, I took Grandma to Grandfather Tue's house

On our way, it rained heavily, and got her socks wet

Afterthat, the rain stopped

We went to the village by Grandfather Tue's car

I helped Grandma walk over the dunes to her great grandfather's grave

Without her socks, her feet kept slipping out of her shoes
